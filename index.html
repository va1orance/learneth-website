of your own material.

Start with what you know—or start from the basics. The tutor plans a sequence around the material and your understanding, rather than treating each heading as a complete lesson.

Your original stays intact. A separate learning-session page accumulates the source, your attempts, and tailored corrections, so you can revisit how your understanding changed.

Assessment focuses on the reasoning you show, not just the final answer. A misconception can lead to a smaller follow-up problem before moving on.

Bring your own space and model

Built on SilverBullet.
Kept in your control.

Learneth is a plug, not a separate hosted learning platform. Model requests go to the endpoint you configure; that provider receives the material and answers included in those requests.

  1. Build the plug

    From the app source, run npm ci and npm run build inside learneth/.

  2. Add it to your space

    Copy learneth.plug.js to SilverBullet’s _plug/ directory and reload.

  3. Choose your model

    Open Learneth: Settings in the command palette. Configure your OpenAI-compatible endpoint and models. API keys are stored in your browser.

  4. Open material and begin

    Run Learneth: Start session. Choose diagnosis, basics, or a custom request. Mid-session, use Learneth: Chat with tutor to ask questions or change direction.

A work in progress, honestly.

Learneth is experimental. AI explanations and grading can be wrong. Model quality matters. PDF import is not yet part of this setup, and handwriting is not a finished feature. This website is a local draft; no public download is available here yet.
